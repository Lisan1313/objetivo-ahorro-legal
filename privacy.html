Objetivo Ahorro privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026 · Leer en español

In short: Objetivo Ahorro collects nothing about you. Everything you log stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no servers of ours, and we never send your information anywhere.

What is stored, and where

The app stores only on your phone what you type into it:

  • The expenses you log: category, amount and date.
  • What you decide not to spend, which goes into the jar.
  • Your savings goals and the ones you have completed.
  • Your preferences: weekly limit, category limits, currency, language, categories, reminders and whether you want the lock on opening.

The app never sends that information anywhere: not to us, not to anyone. We have no servers to receive it, so we have no access to it.

What we do NOT do

  • We never ask for your name, email or any personal detail.
  • There are no user accounts and no sign-up.
  • We use no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.
  • We do not share, sell or transfer data to third parties, because we hold none.
  • We do not connect to your bank or read your real transactions: you log everything yourself.

Backups

The app lets you create a backup file so you do not lose your history when you change phones. You create that file and you decide what happens to it: keep it on your phone, in your cloud storage, or send it to yourself. The app never uploads it on its own. Once you store it in another service, that service's terms apply.

The same goes for exporting your expenses to Excel: you create the file when you ask for it and decide where to keep it or who to send it to.

Android's automatic backup. Separately, Android has its own backup. If it is turned on in your phone's settings, it may store this app's data in your Google account and give it back to you if you reinstall the app or change phones. This is an Android feature, not ours: that backup goes to your account, not to us, and we cannot see it. Google stores it encrypted. If you would rather it did not happen, you can turn it off in your phone's backup settings.

Permissions and sensors

  • Motion sensor: used only so the contents of the jar tilt when you move the phone. Those readings are never stored or sent.
  • Files: only when you tap to save or load a backup, through the system file picker, or to export your expenses.
  • Fingerprint, face or PIN: only if you turn on the lock in Settings. Android checks it: the app only learns whether it succeeded. We never see, store or send your fingerprint, face or PIN.
  • Reminders: the app schedules its reminders on the phone itself. They never go through any server and you can turn them off at any time.

In-app purchases

If you buy the Full Jar, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your card details. Google only tells us whether the purchase is active for your account.

Updates and ratings

When you open it, the app asks Google Play whether there is a new version, so it can let you know. Now and then it may also show Google Play's official window to rate the app. Google Play handles both: we receive no data and do not even know whether you left a rating.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone, whatever their age.

How to delete your data

Two ways, both immediate and permanent:

  • In the app: Settings → Delete my data.
  • By uninstalling the app: everything it kept on the phone goes with it.

There is nothing to request from us, because we hold no copy.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever started handling data differently, we would update this page and say so inside the app before it happens.
